Ahmet Aksoy, Ph.D.

Associate Professor · Computer Science & Cybersecurity

Dr. Aksoy directs NetML Lab at the University of Central Missouri. His research connects network traffic analysis, machine learning, optimization, explainable AI, and large language models to practical cybersecurity problems.
