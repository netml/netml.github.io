Join Our Research Team in Network Security and Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning

We invite applications from dedicated and enthusiastic students eager to explore the cutting-edge fields of Network Security and Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning. We are seeking individuals with a solid foundation in programming and a strong desire to deepen their understanding of theoretical and practical concepts in these rapidly evolving areas.

If you are driven by curiosity and excited to contribute to impactful research, we would love to hear from you. To express your interest, please arrange a meeting by visiting Dr. Aksoy's office or submit your application using the contact information provided below. Your application should include your Curriculum Vitae (CV) and a brief statement outlining your research interests and professional aspirations.

This is more than just an invitation to join a research group — it is an opportunity to be at the forefront of technological innovation within a dynamic and supportive environment. We look forward to the unique perspective and energy you will bring to our team.
